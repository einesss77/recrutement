# Visuel éditorial de l’accueil

Outil : générateur d’images intégré (imagegen). Image originale créée pour cette version du site, puis copiée dans le projet.

Fichier : `public/assets/bm-collective.png`.

Intention : une nature morte éditoriale sur les compétences complémentaires et leur assemblage. Ce visuel conceptuel ne représente ni une équipe B&M, ni un bureau, ni une réalisation client.

Prompt final :

> Create a single landscape 3:2 editorial still-life photograph for B&M Digital Consulting, a premium French technology consultancy website. Subject: physical modular sculptural objects forming an elegant collaborative system on a warm off-white studio table: a tall midnight blue architectural arch bridging two mint-green solid blocks, one translucent pale-blue glass cylinder beside a small group of sage-green and terracotta geometric solids. These are tactile real manufactured objects, not a digital interface or an infographic. Art direction: sophisticated contemporary design magazine / architectural model photography, warm directional sunlight from upper left, rich long diagonal shadows, fine matte ceramic and brushed metal textures, subtle photographic grain, elegantly asymmetric close-up framing, one pale mint translucent acrylic sheet catching the light. Palette midnight #111e2c, mint #b9efde, sage, warm cream and very restrained terracotta. Objects occupy the central two thirds, large sculptural shapes, strong visual presence at small display sizes. No text, no letters, no logos, no people, no screens, no UI, no glowing network nodes, no gradients as a subject. Photorealistic high-end studio still life, intentional and warm, 1536x1024 composition.
